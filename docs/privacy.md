# Metocast privacy policy

Metocast is the control app for a church's own livestream server. This policy covers the Metocast
apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and the Metocast desktop app.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. The apps contain no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and they
send nothing to the developer or to any third party chosen by the developer.

## Where your data goes

- **Your Metocast server.** The iPhone and iPad app connects only to the server address you enter
  or scan. That server belongs to you or your church and runs on your own Mac. Events, slides,
  recordings and settings live there, not with the developer.
- **Services you connect yourself.** When you sign in to YouTube or Facebook, or add a Bible API key,
  in the server's connector settings, the server talks to those services on your behalf, under their
  own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to them until you set them up.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** only to scan the QR code that connects the app to your server. Images are not stored
  or sent anywhere.
- **Local network:** to reach your Metocast server and your livestream equipment.
- **Microphone (Mac):** only when that Mac runs the server and records audio from your mixer. The
  recordings stay on that Mac until you upload them.

The app keeps its own settings, such as the server address and token, on the device.

## Children

The apps are not aimed at children and collect no data from anyone.
